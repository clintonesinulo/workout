30-DAY STRENGTH PROGRAMME

Full body training

Build muscle at home · 3 days per week
Adjustable barbell & body weight

30-day workout schedule

Train three days per week, with a rest day between sessions. Allow 35–50 minutes. Warm up for five minutes before lifting.

Workout A

Days 1, 8, 15, 22 and 29

Split squat · 8–12 per leg
Stand in a staggered stance. Bend both knees and lower your back knee toward the floor. Push through your front foot to stand.

Push-up · 6–12
Place hands just wider than your shoulders. Keep your body straight as you lower your chest. Use a sturdy countertop to make it easier.

Bent-over barbell row · 8–12
Hinge at the hips with soft knees. Hold your torso steady and pull the bar toward your lower ribs.

Barbell Romanian deadlift · 8–12
From standing, soften your knees and move your hips back. Keep the bar close to your legs. Stand tall when you feel a stretch in your hamstrings.

Standing barbell curl · 10–15
Stand tall with palms facing forward. Bend your elbows to lift the bar and lower it under control.

Workout B

Days 3, 10, 17 and 24

Reverse lunge · 8–12 per leg
From standing, step back and bend both knees. Push through your front foot to return. Use a wall for balance if needed.

Pike push-up · 6–10
Place hands on the floor, lift hips into an inverted V and bend your elbows to lower your head slightly forward between your hands. Use a sturdy countertop for an easier variation.

Paused barbell row · 8–12
Hinge at the hips, draw the bar toward your lower ribs, pause for one second and slowly lower.

Single-leg glute bridge · 10–15 per leg
Lie on your back with one foot planted. Lift the other foot and press through the planted foot to raise your hips. Keep your pelvis level.

Single-leg calf raise · 12–20 per leg
Hold a wall for balance. Stand on one leg, rise onto your toes and lower your heel slowly.

Workout C

Days 5, 12, 19 and 26

Paused squat · 12–20
Stand with feet about shoulder-width apart. Bend your hips and knees, pause for a second and stand. Hold a plate at your chest if you can grip it securely.

Paused push-up · 6–12
Keep your body straight and pause briefly with your chest near the floor before pressing up. Use a sturdy countertop if needed.

Bent-over barbell row · 8–12
Push your hips back. Keep your torso still and pull the bar toward your lower ribs, then lower slowly.

Glute bridge · 12–20
Lie on your back with feet planted. Lift your hips and squeeze your glutes for two seconds. Add a securely held plate across your hips when ready.

Side plank · 20–40 seconds per side
Rest on one forearm with elbow below shoulder. Lift your hips and hold a straight line from head to feet. Repeat on the other side.

Weekly progression

Week 1 · Choose a weight that leaves two or three good repetitions in reserve. Start with two sets per exercise.

Week 2 · Add a third set to the first three movements. Keep the same weights as Week 1.

Week 3 · Keep your set counts. Add one repetition where you can maintain good form.

Week 4 · When every set reaches the top of its range with two good repetitions left, add the smallest available weight increment.

Days 29–30 · Repeat Workout A and compare your log with Day 1. Day 30 is a recovery day.
